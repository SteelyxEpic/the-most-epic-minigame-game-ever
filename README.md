A new warioware game appears!

So far there's only 13 minigames(4 are for the prologue of the game whereas the 5th to 10th ones are for the stranded version and the remaining are for the refined version):
Catch the planets:
On the loose! Planets have been seen, your goal is to catch the planets and gain whatever information you can take from them

Bounty hunters aboard!:
Aliens are hunting the same thing as you! Better watch out for them, if not they might just leave you in the dust!

Dodging Asteroids:
Asteroids inbound! Use your incredible maneuvering skills and fly between the flaming hunk of solid rock without crashing!

Sequence reset:
Your ship needs recalibration! Remember the sequence and test your memory because if you don't your journey might just end there

Lever fixer:
Your ship has broken down from the crash! Quick flip your lever controls to get it back running! You better have the code ready!

Fuel Gauge Fanatic:
The urge to refuel! Refuel your tank to the correct amount because if not.....Uhm no clue! (whispers "maybe it'll explode or something :p")

Here to Earth, are you there?!:
Rotate your antenna to the Earth's direction and send help signals, maybe someone will hear you!

Earth Found!:
The satelite's moving on its own! Find where Earth is from the light and press the force stop button quickly!

Meteor shower:
Oh no! A meteor shower's coming! Quick dodge them now!

Lights out!:
The lights are off, find the switch and back on the lights!!

Ore sorting, more smelting!:
Put the ore in and leave the stone out! Make sure to put all the ores in or else you might lose the profits!

Pour me another!:
pour the molten ore into casts! Too little and you're out! Too much and you're out! Too perfect and you're perfect! <3

Drill bit madness!:
Drill into the ore at the right power! Too much power and you might shatter it and too little....well you know what happens when there's too little (nothing~)

Controls:
All minigames will use either your mouse, arrow keys or space bar
